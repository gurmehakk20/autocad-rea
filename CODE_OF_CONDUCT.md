# Code of Conduct

OpenDraft welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. We expect everyone taking part to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific, actionable feedback on code and designs, and separate the work from the person.
- Credit the work of others, and say where an idea came from.
- Respect the licensing rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Do not share code or files you are not allowed to share.
- Keep discussions on topic and avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination of any kind.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the project maintainers through the repository's issue tracker or the contact listed in the repository settings. If a report concerns a maintainer, ask for it to be handled by someone else. Maintainers will review reports in confidence and respond proportionately. Anyone who breaks these expectations may be asked to stop participating.
